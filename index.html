Our story

More Than Just Coffee

Brew & Bean began with a simple idea: a café should feel like your favourite corner of the city. We are a small team in Pune brewing specialty coffee, baking in-house and keeping the door open for whoever walks in — a laptop and a deadline, a first date, or a quiet hour with a book.

We choose our beans with care, grind them fresh for every cup and make our bakes in small batches each morning. Nothing sits under a heat lamp; if it isn't fresh, it doesn't leave the counter. Every plate and every cup is made to be lingered over.

  • Fresh Ingredients

    Small-batch bakes and seasonal produce, prepared every morning.

  • Quality Coffee

    Carefully sourced beans, ground fresh and brewed to order.

  • Cozy Atmosphere

    Warm light, unhurried seating and space to stay a while.
